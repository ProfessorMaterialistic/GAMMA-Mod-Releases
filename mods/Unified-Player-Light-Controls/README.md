# Unified Player Light Controls 2.1.2

Saved OFF / ON / AUTO modes for supported weapon lights and lasers, aiming
policies, tuning and NVG linking. Normal and IR headlamps remain OFF/ON.
Works independently or with One Key's gestures and quick menu.

Weapon-light ADS transition hotfix, promoted from the owner-accepted RC.
