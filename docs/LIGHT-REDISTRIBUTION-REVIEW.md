# Light licenses and source

Light 2.1.0 preserves original-author credits and ships scoped GPL-3.0,
AGPL-3.0 and applicable MIT notices. Modified Soy/Borksy integrations are
distributed with permission for GPL-compatible source copying, modification
and redistribution. Their upstream packages keep their own original terms;
Soy UTLF IR's original Proprietary listing is not described as an open license.

Original Light radial/semantic artwork is generated by the project's drawing
code. Complete host/dependency models, presets, sounds and engine binaries
are separately installed and are not bundled.
